A children's picture book

Whoever heard of a singing duck? Like in João Gilberto's music, its quacks turned into samba and made friends along the way.

Now the duck, the teal, the goose, and the swan spend their days by the lagoon, singing and having fun.

Based on the song by Jayme Silva and Neuza Teixeira, performed by João Gilberto.

The song that inspired the book

For Teresa

This little book was made for Teresa, but other children are welcome to enjoy it too.

Teresa, dad started this book when you were born. It is something special that represents the connection between you, mom, and me. Music is very important in our lives: it is part of how we understand who we are and the world around us.

This duck song was a way for us to be together when you were still in mom's belly, and it also helped us stay calm when things were difficult.

When everything feels a little strange, take a breath and try to take it easy. Maybe a QUEN QUEN will make things more fun.

Berlin, April 2025.

Henrique Müller

Based on the song by Jayme Silva and Neuza Teixeira, performed by João Gilberto.
